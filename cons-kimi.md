# Consolidated Partition

Effective evidence base: 14 passes (cl-kimi-07 returned no groups). Counts below are out of 15.

## A. The partition (14 roots)

1. **protection** — Prevent harm, loss, or misuse to people, property, systems, data, or the vulnerable. Includes physical safety, security/access control, privacy, and safeguarding. *Discriminator vs. care: does the act avert a threat (protection) or improve a condition (care)?* Found in ~14/15 passes.

2. **care** — Sustain, restore, or improve the physical, mental, or social wellbeing of persons or animals. Includes healing, hygiene, counseling, and direct provisioning of food/comfort to dependents. *Discriminator vs. protection: it raises wellbeing rather than blocking a hazard.* ~14/15.

3. **communication** — Make information, expression, or sentiment reach and be understood by others. The end is shared awareness, not any action taken afterward. *Discriminator vs. knowledge: communication transfers what is known; knowledge establishes what is true.* ~14/15.

4. **coordination** — Align people, schedules, resources, and workflows so collective activity can occur. Includes scheduling, planning, and routine administration of meetings and commitments. *Discriminator vs. communication: coordination ends in aligned action/state, not merely in being informed.* ~13/15.

5. **knowledge** — Discover, verify, organize, or preserve facts and insight. Includes investigation, research, testing, monitoring, and trustworthy record-keeping. *Discriminator vs. education: knowledge produces truth; education produces capable people.* ~14/15.

6. **education** — Develop knowledge, skill, or capacity in learners (or oneself). *Discriminator vs. knowledge: the locus of change is a person's ability, not a body of information.* ~9/15.

7. **justice** — Resolve disputes, adjudicate claims, and enforce rights, law, and accountability to reach an authoritative outcome. *Discriminator vs. compliance: justice settles a contested matter; compliance prevents deviation from a standing rule.* ~13/15.

8. **compliance** — Ensure conduct, records, and processes conform to rules, standards, and obligations, with evidence. Includes audit, verification of conformity, and order-keeping/moderation in shared spaces. *Discriminator vs. protection: compliance answers "did we follow the rule?"; protection answers "did we prevent the harm?"* ~14/15.

9. **build** — Create new physical or digital artifacts: construct, manufacture, design, develop software and content artifacts. *Discriminator vs. maintain: build brings something into being; maintain keeps something already being.* ~13/15.

10. **maintain** — Keep existing equipment, facilities, systems, and environments functional, clean, and repaired. *Discriminator vs. build: the artifact's existence is presupposed.* ~13/15.

11. **commerce** — Effect exchanges of value: buying, selling, paying, financing, procuring, settling accounts. *Discriminator vs. coordination: commerce ends in transferred value/ownership, not merely aligned activity.* ~13/15.

12. **move** — Relocate people, goods, or materials through transit and logistics. *Discriminator vs. maintain: the end is a changed location, not a preserved condition.* ~4/15 — **weak** (see D).

13. **expression** — Create and publish cultural, artistic, editorial, or promotional content for an audience; win attention, support, or demand. *Discriminator vs. communication: expression's end is the work's reception or the audience's persuasion, not transfer of needed information.* ~8/15.

14. **union** — Form marriages, partnerships, or family alliances as the terminal outcome. *Discriminator vs. care: the end is a new standing bond between parties, not a person's wellbeing.* ~5/15 — **weak** (see D).

## B. Merges

- **protection** ← safety, security, protecting, privacy, safeguarding, access (control), protect (ds-01), safety+security splits in ds-00/03/04, kimi-03/04/05 protection.
- **care** ← care, heal/healing, health/healthcare, welfare (persons), feed, hospitality (provisioning folded here).
- **communication** ← communication, inform/informing, circulation, "convey messages" halves of ds-05/07 communication groups.
- **coordination** ← coordination, planning, scheduling, administer/administration (routine alignment parts), kimi-01 coordination+planning.
- **knowledge** ← knowledge, information, understanding, discovery, inquiry, investigation, study (research parts), documentation, data stewardship, verifying (fact-confirming parts).
- **education** ← education, teaching, learning, study (instruction parts).
- **justice** ← justice, legal, adjudication, dispute, resolving (dispute/claims parts).
- **compliance** ← compliance, comply, integrity, accountability, moderation, order, membership (rule-enforcement in shared spaces), verifying (conformity parts), govern (ds-01, rule-keeping parts).
- **build** ← production (creation parts), build, making, development, engineering, design, creation, construction.
- **maintain** ← maintenance, stewardship, repair, operate (physical-infrastructure parts).
- **commerce** ← commerce, exchange, transaction, finance, resource, procuring/procurement, trade.
- **move** ← logistics, move, moving, transport.
- **expression** ← expression, publishing, promotion/promote, persuasion.
- **union** ← match/matchmaking, union, kinship, matching (marriage parts).

## C. Dropped

- **representation / acting-for / liaison / diplomacy-by-capacity** — flagged unplaced in nearly every pass; fails the standing-relation screen. The end belongs to the principal.
- **operating / platform-state maintenance / participation / reliability / CRUD / "get/insert/relay X"** — mechanisms over shared digital state; no ultimate end survives stripping (ds-06, ds-00, kimi-00 "operating," kimi-05/06 "participation," kimi-06 "reliability").
- **governance/administration as enterprise-oversight** (kimi-04 governance, ds-03 governance directive parts) — direction of work is a means to the work's end; enforcement parts went to compliance, alignment parts to coordination.
- **diplomacy** (ds-07, 1 pass) — single-act artifact; its acts distribute to communication/commerce/justice.
- **staffing/matching-to-roles** (ds-02, kimi-03 matching parts) — a means to coordination/commerce; only marriage-matching survived as union.
- **environment** (ds-02, ds-04, ds-07) — genuine end (sustain ecological systems) but thin (3 passes, few acts); arguably a subdomain of care/protection. Dropped for lack of evidence, noted in E.
- **sentiment formation, arrival/reporting, "being defeated," bare consent/refusal moves** — outcomes, internal events, or context-dependent speech acts with no intrinsic telos.

## D. Weak roots

- **move** (~4 passes) — real ultimate end but rarely exercised; retained because it merges with nothing cleanly.
- **union** (~5 passes) — recurring but narrow; likely an artifact of repeated synthetic matchmaking acts in the corpus generator.
- **environment** — dropped, but it is the strongest candidate for a 15th root if the corpus grows.

## E. What this corpus cannot tell you

The corpus is dominated by workplace/platform/service acts. It never exercises: **play/recreation** (leisure as its own end — "social" in ds-05 gestures at it), **worship/ritual**, **governance of polities** (legislation, voting, war, diplomacy as ends), **ecological stewardship** at scale, **reproduction/kinship-care** beyond matchmaking, **personal self-realization** (acts done for one's own growth or expression outside any institutional role), and **survival/subsistence** acts (foraging, shelter-building for self). A general taxonomy of human acts would need all of these; no pass saw them.
