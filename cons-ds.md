I treat the 15 supplied sheets as the independent passes: `cl-ds-00`–`cl-ds-07` and `cl-kimi-00`–`cl-kimi-06`.

## A. The partition

**care**  
Sustain or restore physical, mental, social, or animal wellbeing.  
Discriminator vs safety: *Is the end to improve/sustain wellbeing, or to prevent a specific harm?*  
Found by 15/15 passes.

**safety**  
Prevent harm, injury, abuse, neglect, fire, accident, or dangerous conditions to people, property, or environments.  
Discriminator vs security: *Is the harm physical/accidental, or logical/unauthorized?*  
Found by 15/15 passes.

**security**  
Protect systems, data, identity, access, and informational boundaries from misuse or unauthorized entry.  
Discriminator vs safety: *Is the threat physical, or digital/authorization-based?*  
Found by ~8/15 passes.

**know**  
Produce, verify, organize, retrieve, model, or discover factual understanding and evidence.  
Discriminator vs inform: *Is the end to create/verify understanding, or to convey awareness to an audience?*  
Found by 15/15 passes.

**inform**  
Transmit information, expression, public messages, announcements, or persuasive content so others know or respond.  
Discriminator vs educate: *Does the recipient merely become aware, or gain durable skill/capacity?*  
Found by 15/15 passes.

**educate**  
Develop knowledge, skill, competence, or capability through teaching, training, assessment, or demonstration.  
Discriminator vs know: *Is the end to build another’s capacity, or to establish knowledge itself?*  
Found by ~8/15 passes.

**exchange**  
Complete economic transactions and manage money, finance, assets, procurement, supply, pricing, funding, or market access.  
Discriminator vs produce: *Does the act transfer value/ownership, or bring an artifact into being?*  
Found by ~14/15 passes.

**produce**  
Create, build, design, assemble, develop, or construct physical, digital, technical, or prepared artifacts.  
Discriminator vs maintain: *Is the end to create something new, or to keep an existing thing functional/standard?*  
Found by ~14/15 passes.

**maintain**  
Keep equipment, facilities, infrastructure, systems, documentation, records, and natural/built environments functional, clean, reliable, or sustainable.  
Discriminator vs produce: *Preserve/repair/restore, or create/build?*  
Found by ~14/15 passes.

**coordinate**  
Align people, schedules, resources, workflows, meetings, commitments, and organizational routines so joint activity can happen.  
Discriminator vs govern: *Is the aim voluntary alignment of activity, or rule-based moderation of a shared space?*  
Found by 15/15 passes.

**comply**  
Ensure acts, records, controls, risks, and claims conform to rules, standards, obligations, or evidence.  
Discriminator vs justice: *Is the end prospective conformity, or retrospective resolution of a disputed rights/legal matter?*  
Found by ~13/15 passes.

**justice**  
Resolve disputes, determine liability/eligibility/rights, administer legal process, custody, remedies, or formal adjudication.  
Discriminator vs govern: *Is this a formal legal/dispute resolution, or ongoing community/system moderation?*  
Found by ~11/15 passes.

**govern**  
Maintain orderly participation, membership, access, roles, content standards, and moderation in shared physical or digital spaces.  
Discriminator vs security: *Is the object a social/community boundary, or a system/data/access boundary?*  
Found by ~11/15 passes.

**move**  
Move people, goods, vehicles, or physical materials through transit, logistics, or circulation.  
Discriminator vs exchange: *Does the end change physical location, or transfer value/ownership?*  
Found by ~4/15 passes. Weak root; see D.

## B. Merges

**care** ← care, heal, healing, health, healthcare, wellbeing, assist, assisting, welfare, rehabilitate, feed, hospitality, social/emotional wellbeing, parts of safeguarding/matching/community.  
**safety** ← safety, protection, protecting, protect, safeguarding, fire/hazard suppression, accident prevention.  
**security** ← security, privacy, logical access control, cyber protection, identity protection.  
**know** ← knowledge, information, discovery, inquiry, study, understanding, investigation, research, verification, evidence-based integrity, data analysis.  
**inform** ← communication, communicating, informing, publishing, expression, promotion/awareness, persuasion, public relations, announcements, document/message circulation.  
**educate** ← education, teaching, learning, training, instruction, demonstration for skill-building.  
**exchange** ← commerce, exchange, transaction, finance, money, procurement, resource/money/assets, marketing/sales/fundraising, billing, settlement.  
**produce** ← production, making, construction, build, development, creation, design, engineering, assembly, content/artifact creation.  
**maintain** ← maintenance, stewardship, repair, reliability, upkeep, quality/standards, data stewardship, environment/sustainability, grounds/facilities upkeep.  
**coordinate** ← coordination, administration, operations, scheduling, planning, staffing, event logistics, workflow alignment.  
**comply** ← compliance, accountability, integrity/audit, rule-conformance, risk limits, quality assurance where standards-based.  
**justice** ← justice, legal, adjudication, resolving, dispute, litigation, custody, enforcement, rights/liability.  
**govern** ← governance, order, moderation, membership, participation, community standards, role/access moderation.  
**move** ← logistics, moving, transportation, physical circulation; not message circulation, which goes to inform.

## C. Dropped

Dropped as standing relations, not ultimate purposes: representation, acting on behalf of, liaison, fiduciary capacity, diplomatic role, sub-delegation, advocate for a represented person, report back to principal, arrange representation, route mail/write for principal.

Dropped as bare mechanism or CRUD without stated end: many data/API operations, “get/build/request X,” retrieval primitives, insert-at-beginning, read-only lookups, generic object create/remove/listen, accept/decline protocol moves, some record updates.

Dropped as non-purposes or outcomes: privacy breach, being defeated, arrival at work, absence/boundary, forbidden scope violation, bare sentiment formation with no further outcome.

## D. Weak roots

**move** is the weakest chosen root: independently named in only ~4/15 passes. The corpus under-represents physical logistics and transit.  
**environment** appears as a separate group in only ~3/15 passes; I folded it into **maintain** rather than granting root status.  
Matchmaking/kinship/community appears in a few passes but was folded into **care**/**coordinate**/**govern** because its boundaries were unstable.

## E. What this corpus cannot tell you

A general taxonomy of acts would need roots this corpus barely or never exercised: worship/ritual, play/recreation, aesthetic art-for-art, military/defense action, reproduction/parenting, political representation, pure science vs applied science, and independent ecological restoration. The corpus over-represents digital, administrative, compliance, and coordination acts, and under-represents physical, familial, civic, and ecological ends.
