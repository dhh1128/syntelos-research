# T4 — the frozen telos partition

Frozen 2026-08-24. This is the **semantic contract**: extensions and outward tests. Labels are
provisional handles and T5 may replace any of them without reopening this document — that is the
point of freezing semantics separately from names.

Every node's test is present-tense and structural, per [D-6PVH rev.2]: it turns on features of the
act at the time it is performed, or on an outcome the act causally determines through mechanism.

## One structural change made while freezing

`instruct` and `promote` were carried through T2/T3 as roots. Under the species rule [D-7NKQ] they
are not: both **transmit content to an identified audience** and so satisfy the parent test, each
adding structure on top. Instruction is communication plus pedagogical apparatus; promotion is
communication plus sponsorship. Making them children is what the rule requires, and it also puts the
`communicate`/`instruct`/`promote` boundary — the one Daniel flagged as an over-attraction risk —
inside a single branch where the counter-examples can do their work.

Flagged rather than slipped in: this reduces the root count from twelve to ten.

recurrence.

## What was removed, and why

| removed | reason |
|---|---|
| `ecology` | Subject area, not a purpose. Fails the verb test; "is the object a natural system" is observable, which is what makes it a subject area. Acts redistribute to `know`, `provide`, `protect`. |
| `move` | Mechanism, not a purpose. Relocation serves care, availability, protection, exchange — a node appearing under many purposes is a mechanism. |
| `secure` | Merged into `protect`. Accidental vs adversarial is a threat-model property of the *target*. |
| `maintain` | Merged into `provide`. Create vs preserve is already the effect facet; splitting here duplicates one distinction on two orthogonal axes. |
| `representing` | Standing relation, not a purpose. Belongs on the relation facet. |

## Boundaries that need counter-examples in T6

Recorded now so T6 cannot forget them. Each is a place where ordinary language scatters across nodes:

- **know / conform / judge.** *Assess*, *evaluate*, *review* are everyday synonyms landing on three
  different nodes. Measure a sample → `know`. Peer-review a paper or grade against a rubric →
  `conform`. Rule on a disputed claim → `judge`.
- **communicate / instruct / promote.** Explaining something is not teaching it, and neither is
  advertising, but all three transmit content.
- **provide / care.** Feeding a person is `care`; feeding a boiler is `provide`. The object decides.
- **belong / unite / coordinate.** Introducing two people is `coordinate`; admitting a member is
  `belong`; marrying them is `unite`.
- **transfer / trade / give.** And theft, which is `transfer` with the consent requisite *absent* —
  not a species, a validity defect.

## Standing exclusions

Stated so a reader knows where the vocabulary stops rather than discovering it:

- **Authority that inheres in the actor** — rights, standing, capacity — is out of scope. Syntelos
  describes acts and the authority a grantor confers over them.
- **Acts on living subjects have no state-kind coordinate.** `care` is the most strongly evidenced
  root in the set and cannot be located on the state-kind axis, whose six values contain nothing a
  person or animal can be. Backlogged to the state-kind facet, orthogonal to this freeze.
