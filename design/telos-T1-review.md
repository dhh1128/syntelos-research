# T1 review — the derived telos partition

Prepared 2026-08-22 for Daniel's inspection. This is the reviewable form of phase T1: three
independent consolidations, aligned **by concept rather than by name**, since no two of them used
the same labels. Labels below are whatever each seat emitted and carry no weight — naming is T5.

## How to give feedback

Every concept has a number, `C1`–`C15`. Reply by number: *"C8 merge, C13 keep separate, C14 drop."*
Or edit the **Verdict** line under any concept in place and tell me you have — I will read the file
back rather than assume.

The six unanimous concepts probably need no more than a skim. **The nine contested ones are the
review**, and they are where the partition is actually undecided.

## Provenance

Eight batches of ~135 acts from the 1,076-act domain-balanced sample. Each batch clustered
independently by two seats, blind to every other batch and to each other — 15 usable passes. Those
15 were then consolidated three separate times, by `codex`, `ds`, and `kimi`, each blind to the
others. Full texts: `design/sources/telos3-consolidation-{codex,ds,kimi}.md`; the 15 batch passes
are in `design/sources/telos-batches/`.

"**n/15**" below means how many of the 15 blind passes independently recovered that concept. It is
the closest thing to evidence this exercise produces. A low number is not necessarily weak — a
concept only 3% of the corpus exercises cannot appear in slices that contain none of it — but it
does mean fewer independent looks agreed.

## Part 1 — Unanimous. All three consolidations found these as distinct roots.

**C1 · wellbeing of persons and animals** — 14–15/15. `care` in all three.
Restore or sustain physical, mental, or social wellbeing. Treatment, welfare, safeguarding.
> *apply protective coating of fluoride to teeth* · *educate or advise clients on animal health
> care, nutrition, or behavior problems*

**C3 · establishing what is true** — 14–15/15. `knowledge` / `know`.
Discover, investigate, verify, analyse, retrieve. The end is that something true becomes known.

**C5 · conveying to an audience** — 12–15/15. `communication` / `communicate`.
Success is receipt and shared awareness, not verified understanding (C3) and not changed disposition
(C6).

**C7 · aligning joint activity** — 10–14/15. `coordination` / `coordinate`.
Schedules, resources, commitments, workflows, so collective activity can occur. One seat flagged
that some passes suspected this of being a means rather than an end, and retained it because the
arranged event is itself terminal for the arranger.

**C9 · transferring value** — 12–14/15. `exchange` / `commerce`.
Buying, selling, paying, settling, financing, procuring.

**C12 · belonging and recognised presence** — 9–10/15. `participation` / `social` / `membership`.
Who may belong, enter, and act in a shared space. Moderation, roles, onboarding, community life.

## Part 2 — Contested. This is the review.

> **A third answer is available for C4, C6 and C13, added 2026-08-22.** Daniel's reading is that
> these are one act at two causal horizons rather than two purposes, and I agree — see
> `design/horizon-and-depth.md`. The resolution is neither merge nor split but **depth**: the further
> horizon becomes a child of the nearer one, which is what v1's hierarchy is for. So for those three,
> a verdict of "child" is as available as "merge" or "separate". C2, C8, C10/C11, C14 and C15 are
> unaffected — they differ in source, effect, termination, or object rather than in horizon.

### C2 · preventing harm — one root or two?

| | reading | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `codex` | **one** root, `protection` — all harm prevention together | 14/15 |
| `ds` | **two**: `protect` (accident, hazard, neglect) vs `secure` (adversary, intrusion, misuse) | 14/15, 13/15 |
| `kimi` | **two**: `safety` vs `security`, same cut | 13/15, 10/15 |

Two of three split it, on whether the threat is **accidental or adversarial**. The split has real
consequence for delegation: guarding against an adversary is a different authority than guarding
against an accident.

### C13 · durable pairing — distinct from belonging (C12)?

`codex` separates `union` (5/15): *bring particular parties into a durable pairing or alliance,

role's further purpose?*

`ds` folds it into `social`; `kimi` folds it into `membership`.

This is the one you predicted and the reason `/relate` was retained. All three consolidations found
*something* relational (C12), but only one isolated **pairing** from **belonging**. Worth deciding
on the theory, since 5/15 cannot settle it either way.

## Part 4 — Where a verdict leads

T2 verbing pass (a semantic check, since a root that resists verbing is usually mis-drawn) → T3
resolve whatever remains contested → T4 freeze → T5 naming, against `design/naming-criteria.md` →
T6 write the roots into `taxonomy/telos/` as registry nodes with discriminators and counter-examples,
at which point the linter starts enforcing them.
