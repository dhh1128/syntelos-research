Below are the outputs of 15 independent clustering passes. Each pass saw a different ~135-act slice of the same 1,076-act corpus, and no pass saw any other's output. Each grouped its slice by TELOS — the purpose an act serves, the outcome that is its reason for existing.

Your task: consolidate these into a SINGLE partition of 8 to 14 root categories.

## How to consolidate

- Groups that recur across many passes under different names are the same root. Merge them and pick the best name.
- A group appearing in only one or two passes is weak evidence. It may be a genuine root the other slices happened not to contain, or it may be an artifact of one slice. Decide which, and say which.
- Watch for the same name used for different things, and different names used for the same thing. Both occur.
- Report, for each root, roughly how many of the 15 passes independently found it. That number is your evidence.

## Constraints on the result

- **Roots must be ULTIMATE purposes.** A purpose that is always a means to a further end is not a root — strip it away and ask whether an end remains. "Represent a client" fails: the end is whatever is represented toward.
- **Standing relations between actors are NOT purposes.** Acting *on behalf of*, *in the capacity of*, *as agent for* describes who an actor is to someone else, not what they are trying to bring about. Exclude such candidates; they belong on a different axis.
- **Purposes, not mechanisms.** "Read operations" is a mechanism.
- **Mutually exclusive**, and collectively exhaustive over what these passes saw.
- For each root, state the discriminating question that separates it from its nearest neighbour. If you cannot state one, merge them.

## Output

**A. The partition.** Per root: name (one lowercase word), a two-sentence definition, the discriminating question against its nearest neighbour, and how many of the 15 passes found it.

**B. Merges.** Which input group names collapsed into which root, so the mapping is auditable.

**C. Dropped.** Any input group you excluded, and why — especially any excluded by the ultimate-purpose or standing-relation screens.

**D. Weak roots.** Roots resting on evidence from only one or two passes, flagged as such.

**E. What this corpus cannot tell you.** Purposes a general taxonomy of acts would need that no pass exercised.

Do not emit a per-act table. Be dense; under 1,600 words.
