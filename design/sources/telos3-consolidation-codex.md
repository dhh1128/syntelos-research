## A. The partition

Evidence counts treat the empty `cl-kimi-07` as a pass finding nothing; thus the practical maximum observed recurrence is 14/15.

1. **care** — Preserve or improve a person’s or animal’s health, development, comfort, dignity, or welfare. It includes treatment, personal support, safeguarding vulnerable people, and rehabilitation.  
   **Discriminator:** Is the intended outcome someone’s wellbeing, rather than merely preventing an external hazard? **Found in ~14/15 passes.**

2. **protection** — Prevent injury, loss, contamination, intrusion, abuse, or system compromise. It covers physical safety, security, privacy, access control, fire prevention, and threat suppression.  
   **Discriminator:** Is success the absence of harm or unauthorized exposure, rather than positive improvement in wellbeing? **~14/15.**

3. **justice** — Determine and give effect to rights, liability, eligibility, remedies, or authoritative outcomes. It includes adjudication, dispute resolution, legal process, custody, and enforcement.  
   **Discriminator:** Must a contested or legally consequential question be authoritatively resolved, rather than merely checked for conformity? **~10/15.**

4. **conformity** — Make conduct, records, products, or processes satisfy applicable rules and warranted standards. It includes compliance, auditability, integrity, verification, accountability, and quality assurance.  
   **Discriminator:** Is there already a standard against which something is tested, rather than a dispute whose rightful outcome must be decided? **~13/15.**

5. **knowledge** — Cause reliable understanding to exist by discovering, testing, organizing, documenting, teaching, or making information retrievable. It includes research, inquiry, education, assessment, and durable records.  
   **Discriminator:** Is success a justified state of understanding, rather than simply getting a message to an audience? **~14/15.**

6. **communication** — Make information or expression reach and become available to its intended audience. It includes informing, publishing, reporting, circulation, protocol communication, and artistic expression.  
   **Discriminator:** Is success receipt or shared awareness, rather than changing the audience’s preference or conduct? **~14/15.**

7. **persuasion** — Change attention, preference, support, demand, recruitment, or public commitment. It includes promotion, publicity, fundraising appeals, advocacy, and audience development.  
   **Discriminator:** Would neutral transmission still succeed, or must recipients become more favorable or more likely to act? **~6/15.**

8. **coordination** — Bring actors, commitments, decisions, and resources into an ordered arrangement that enables collective activity. Scheduling, administration, planning, supervision, and workflow management belong here only when organized collective functioning is itself the stated outcome.  
   **Discriminator:** Is the result organized joint action, rather than membership in the group or delivery of a material resource? **~14/15.**

9. **participation** — Establish and sustain belonging, identity, access, or socially recognized presence in a community or shared space. It includes membership governance, moderation, social connection, hospitality, and community life.  
   **Discriminator:** Is the outcome who may belong and interact, rather than how participants coordinate a particular undertaking? **~9/15.**

10. **provision** — Put usable artifacts, infrastructure, goods, spaces, or services into the required condition and place. It subsumes making, building, operating, repairing, maintaining, transporting, feeding, and technical reliability when their terminal outcome is practical availability.  
    **Discriminator:** Is success that something usable is available, rather than that ownership or payment has changed hands? **~14/15.**

11. **exchange** — Complete a reciprocal transfer of goods, services, money, obligations, or market access. It includes commerce, transactions, finance, procurement, settlement, and service agreements.  
    **Discriminator:** Is reciprocal value or obligation transferred, rather than merely producing or delivering the thing exchanged? **~12/15.**

12. **union** — Bring particular parties into a durable pairing or alliance, especially marriage or kinship. It is narrower than generic matching: the relationship formed is itself the terminal outcome.  
    **Discriminator:** Is a continuing bond the end, rather than selection for a role, which serves the role’s further purpose? **~5/15.**

13. **ecology** — Sustain or restore ecosystems, land, energy, or natural-resource conditions. This is distinct from protecting individual people or assets because the ecological system is the beneficiary.  
    **Discriminator:** Is the valued endpoint environmental condition itself, rather than human safety or maintained infrastructure? **~3/15.**

## B. Merges

- **care:** care, heal/healing, health/healthcare, welfare, safeguarding, support, rehabilitate.
- **protection:** safety, security, protection/protecting, privacy, access.
- **justice:** justice, legal, adjudication, dispute, resolving where resolution was legal or contested.
- **conformity:** compliance, integrity, accountability, quality, verifying; parts of governance and resolving concerned with standards or audit.
- **knowledge:** knowledge, information, understanding, discovery, inquiry/investigation, study, learning, education/teaching, documentation, data stewardship.
- **communication:** communication, informing, publishing, circulation, expression; informational portions of advocacy and support.
- **persuasion:** promote/promotion, persuasion, advocacy, publicity, recruiting, promotional portions of commerce and representation.
- **coordination:** coordination, administration/administer, planning, scheduling, staffing, operational governance, supervision.
- **participation:** participation, membership, order, moderation, govern/governance of communities, community, social, hospitality.
- **provision:** production, making, build, construction, creation/design, maintenance/repair, stewardship, operate/operations, reliability, development/engineering, logistics/move/moving, feed.
- **exchange:** commerce, exchange, transaction, finance, procurement, resource management where directed to economic transfer.
- **union:** match/matching/matchmaking, union, kinship. Job or resource matching was reassigned to the ultimate purpose of the placement.
- **ecology:** environment and environmental stewardship.

## C. Dropped

- **representation, diplomacy-as-capacity, liaison, delegation, fiduciary capacity, acting on behalf of:** dropped as standing relations. Particular acts performed through those relations map by their actual outcome—often persuasion, communication, justice, coordination, or exchange.
- **Generic operations, CRUD, read operations, retrieval, routing, “create/get/remove an object,” API/data primitives:** dropped where no terminal outcome was stated. These are mechanisms, not purposes.
- **Bare planning, coordination, referral, arrival, presence, consent, reservation, and question-asking:** dropped when the act only enabled an unstated further end.
- **Role selection/staffing as an independent root:** dropped because filling a role serves whatever that role exists to achieve.
- **Being defeated, absence, privacy breach, forbidden scope violation, opaque predicates:** dropped because they are states, failures, violations, or uninterpretable descriptions rather than affirmative purposes.
- **Diplomacy as a separate root:** peaceful interstate relations could be a genuine purpose, but only one clear act supported it; the represented-state capacity itself is excluded. The exercised outcome is absorbed into coordination or union-like relationship formation.

## D. Weak roots

No retained root rests on only one or two passes. **Ecology** is the weakest retained root at roughly 3/15, but its coherent beneficiary and repeated independent recovery justify keeping it. **Union** is also relatively sparse (~5/15) but unusually well-defined and repeatedly isolated.

## E. What this corpus cannot tell you

The slices provide little or no evidence for several purposes a general taxonomy may require: recreation or pleasure pursued for its own sake; spiritual worship or transcendence distinct from teaching; reproduction and childbearing; scientific exploration where discovery has no practical brief; political conquest or domination; punishment or vengeance as ends distinct from justice; personal autonomy or self-expression not directed to an audience; rest; aesthetic appreciation without creation; and deliberate destruction, sabotage, or other harmful purposes. Their absence here is evidence about corpus coverage, not evidence that they are not roots.
