**A. The partition**  
Counts are of the 15 unique passes.

- **care** — Preserve or restore physical, mental, or social wellbeing of persons or animals. Includes healing, welfare, and direct assistance. Nearest neighbour **protect**: end is a restored/flourishing condition rather than avoidance of an injurious event. Passes: 15/15.

- **protect** — Prevent harm from accidents, hazards, neglect, or physical threat. Includes safety, safeguarding, fire prevention, and non-adversarial risk reduction. Nearest neighbour **secure**: threat is accidental/neglectful rather than intentional misuse. Passes: 14/15.

- **secure** — Prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, tampering, or misuse of systems, data, identity, and premises. Includes privacy and access control. Nearest neighbour **protect**: adversarial or intentional threat rather than accidental harm. Passes: 13/15.

- **know** — Establish, verify, retrieve, or transmit accurate factual understanding. Includes discovery, inquiry, investigation, research, testing, and information retrieval. Nearest neighbour **communicate**: aim is correct mental representation rather than shared attention or being heard. Passes: 15/15.

- **communicate** — Convey information, expression, persuasion, or public visibility so others receive meaning or attention. Includes informing, publishing, promotion, expression, and advisory speech. Nearest neighbour **know**: end is reception/attention rather than verified understanding. Passes: 15/15.

- **coordinate** — Align people, schedules, resources, workflows, and shared system state so joint activity can occur. Includes administration, planning, scheduling, logistics-like alignment, and routine operations. Nearest neighbour **govern**: align actors/resources for action rather than enforce rule-conformity. Passes: 14/15.

- **produce** — Bring physical or digital artifacts, designs, content, or technical systems into being. Includes building, construction, creation, design, software development, and manufacturing. Nearest neighbour **maintain**: create something new rather than keep an existing thing functional. Passes: 15/15.

- **maintain** — Keep equipment, facilities, infrastructure, records, and systems usable, clean, reliable, and fit for purpose. Includes repair, stewardship, reliability, some operations, and quality/defect resolution. Nearest neighbour **produce**: preserve function rather than create a new artifact. Passes: 14/15.

- **exchange** — Complete transfers of value, goods, services, money, or market access. Includes commerce, finance, transactions, procurement, selling, settling, and resource movement tied to economic exchange. Nearest neighbour **produce**: transfer value or ownership rather than create an artifact. Passes: 14/15.

- **govern** — Ensure conformity to law, policy, standards, rights, or shared rules; resolve disputes and enforce obligations. Includes compliance, justice, legal process, moderation, accountability, adjudication, and integrity. Nearest neighbour **coordinate**: enforce norms rather than align actors/resources. Passes: 15/15.

- **educate** — Develop a learner’s knowledge, skill, capacity, or competence through instruction, assessment, or demonstration. Includes teaching, learning, and training. Nearest neighbour **know**: build a person’s capacity rather than establish a fact or body of knowledge. Passes: 9/15.

- **social** — Create or sustain belonging, relationships, shared presence, kinship, or community participation. Includes matchmaking, union formation, hospitality, membership, participation, and community. Nearest neighbour **care**: end is relationship/belonging rather than individual wellbeing. Passes: 10/15.

- **move** — Relocate people, goods, vehicles, animals, or materials from one place or stage to another. Includes physical logistics, transport, and freight movement. Nearest neighbour **exchange**: end is change of location rather than transfer of value or ownership. Passes: 5/15.

- **environment** — Sustain or restore ecological systems, land, energy, or natural resource conditions. Includes environmental study, remediation, and conservation. Nearest neighbour **maintain**: object is natural ecological system rather than built or physical equipment. Passes: 4/15.

**B. Merges**

- **care**: care, heal, healing, health, healthcare, wellbeing, welfare, assisting, rehabilitation.
- **protect**: safety, safeguarding, protection, fire/hazard prevention; part of “safety/security” groups concerned with accidental harm.
- **secure**: security, access, privacy, data protection, identity protection; part of “safety/security” groups concerned with misuse or unauthorized access.
- **know**: knowledge, information, understanding, discovery, inquiry, investigation, study, research, testing, data stewardship where the end is factual reliability.
- **communicate**: communication, informing, inform, publishing, promotion, persuasion, expression, circulation of messages/announcements.
- **coordinate**: coordination, administration, planning, scheduling, operations, resource management where the end is alignment/workflow.
- **produce**: production, build, building, making, construction, creation, design, development, engineering, publishing artifacts.
- **maintain**: maintenance, stewardship, repair, reliability, quality, some operations, some data custody where the end is continued fitness.
- **exchange**: commerce, finance, transaction, exchange, procurement, procuring, resource transfer, selling, settling accounts.
- **govern**: compliance, justice, legal, governance, order, moderation, accountability, integrity, adjudication, dispute resolution, rule enforcement.
- **educate**: education, learning, teaching, training; portions of “study” where the end is learner capacity.
- **social**: community, social, participation, membership, kinship, union, matchmaking, match, hospitality; portions of moderation/access where the end is belonging.
- **move**: logistics, moving, transport, freight, physical circulation of goods.
- **environment**: environment, ecological conditions, environmental stewardship.

Input groups named “operations” repeatedly split: routine alignment went to **coordinate**; system availability/reliability went to **maintain** or **secure**. “Protection” split between **protect** and **secure** depending on whether the threat was accidental or adversarial.

**C. Dropped**

- All standing-relation or representation groups: acting *on behalf of*, agent, delegate, liaison, diplomatic role, broker, principal’s stead, representation, fiduciary capacity, advocacy defined only as representing another. These are actor-status, not terminal purpose.
- Mechanism-only or CRUD-style groups with no stated further end: get/update/delete/create object, read operations, data/API primitives, “object” verbs, routing mail/writing for a principal as bare capacity.
- Outcomes rather than purposes: being defeated, privacy breach, arrival at work, forming one’s private opinion, absence/boundary.
- Opaque or forbidden acts with no legitimate ultimate purpose: share private information as a violation, unspecified status pledges, unclear acronyms.

**D. Weak roots**

- **move** — found in only 5/15 passes. It is a genuine end in transport/logistics slices, but weak overall.
- **environment** — found in only 4/15 passes. Genuine but thinly represented.
- **educate** and **social** are moderate evidence, not weak, but they are the first candidates to collapse if the partition is forced down to 8 roots: **educate** → **know**, **social** → **care** or **communicate**, **move** → **exchange/maintain**, **environment** → **maintain**.

**E. What this corpus cannot tell you**

Religious worship or ritual, military/defense operations, reproduction/parenting beyond union formation, play/sport/leisure, aesthetic contemplation, and political representation as a terminal end were not exercised. Standing-relation acts, which a general taxonomy would need on a separate axis, were deliberately excluded here.
