# Consolidated Partition

## A. The Partition (14 roots)

**1. care** — Restore or sustain the physical, mental, or social wellbeing of people or animals. Includes treatment, hygiene, comfort, dignity, and safeguarding the vulnerable. *Discriminator vs. safety: care improves or maintains a person's condition; safety prevents an adverse event from occurring at all.* Found in ~15 of 15 passes (ds-00 "care," ds-01 "heal," ds-06 "healthcare"/"safeguarding," k-06 "health"/"welfare," etc.).

**2. safety** — Prevent accidental harm, injury, fire, contamination, or system failure to people, property, or operations. The end is a hazard that never materializes. *Discriminator vs. security: the threat is accident or hazard, not an adversary's intent.* Found in ~13 passes.

**3. security** — Protect systems, data, spaces, and identities from unauthorized access or deliberate misuse; verify authorization. *Discriminator vs. safety: there is an adversary or misuser whose intent must be defeated; vs. membership, security excludes actors, membership administers legitimate participants.* Found in ~10 passes (ds-00, ds-02 "privacy," ds-06 "access," k-00, k-03..k-06).

**4. knowledge** — Produce, verify, discover, or retrieve information, facts, and insight — research, investigation, diagnosis, analysis. The end is that something true becomes known. *Discriminator vs. communication: knowledge creates or establishes the content; communication only moves it. Vs. education: knowledge's end is the knowing itself, not a learner's durable capacity.* Found in ~14 passes (ds-02 "discovery," k-01/k-02 "investigation/inquiry," etc.).

**5. education** — Develop durable knowledge, skill, or capacity in learners through instruction, demonstration, or assessment. *Discriminator vs. knowledge: the end is a changed person, not a produced fact.* Found in ~9 passes.

**6. communication** — Convey information, messages, or expression between people so that someone becomes informed or meaning is shared. *Discriminator vs. knowledge: it transmits what is already known; vs. coordination: its end is shared awareness, not aligned action.* Found in ~12 passes.

**7. coordination** — Align people, schedules, resources, and commitments so collective activity can occur — arranging meetings, events, appointments, workflows. *Discriminator vs. communication: the end is synchronized action in time and resources, not merely shared understanding.* Found in ~10 passes. (Caveat: several passes, e.g. ds-03, suspected coordination of being a means; it is retained because the aligned collective event is itself a terminal outcome for the arranger, and ~10 passes independently converged on it.)

**8. compliance** — Ensure conduct, records, and claims conform to rules, standards, agreements, and honesty requirements; audit and attest. *Discriminator vs. justice: compliance prevents or detects deviation prospectively/retrospectively within an organization; justice authoritatively resolves a contested matter. Vs. security: the threat is rule-violation or falsehood, not intrusion.* Found in ~14 passes (ds-02 "integrity," k-00 "verifying," k-02 "accountability," k-03 "integrity").

**9. justice** — Administer legal process: resolve disputes, determine rights, liability, eligibility, and enforce law through adjudication and remedy. *Discriminator vs. compliance: justice issues a binding resolution of a contested claim; compliance keeps ongoing conduct within bounds.* Found in ~9 passes (ds-02, ds-05, ds-06 "legal," k-01/k-02 "adjudication," k-03..k-06).

**10. commerce** — Effect exchanges of value: buying, selling, paying, settling, financing, procuring, and managing money and obligations. *Discriminator vs. coordination: commerce's end is a completed transfer of value; coordination only arranges the conditions for activity.* Found in ~12 passes (ds-02 "finance," ds-07 "exchange," k-01 "transaction," k-02 "finance").

**11. artifacts** — Bring physical or digital things into being, move them, and keep them functional — build, repair, maintain, transport, operate equipment and software. Merged from production/maintenance/logistics/development because passes split this territory inconsistently (e.g. kimi-00 "making"+"moving," kimi-03 "repair"+"engineering") and the shared end is the existence and serviceability of artifacts. *Discriminator vs. care: the object is a thing, not a person.* Found in ~15 passes.

**12. membership** — Govern who may enter, belong, and act within a shared space or community: moderation, roles, accounts, onboarding, removal. *Discriminator vs. security: membership administers legitimate participants' standing; security repels illegitimate intrusion.* Found in ~9 passes (ds-01 "govern," ds-02 "order," ds-06 "moderation," k-01 "governance," k-02 "membership," k-05/k-06 "participation").

**13. promotion** *(weak)* — Win attention, support, recruits, customers, or funding for a person, product, or cause. *Discriminator vs. communication: the end is changed disposition or behavior in the audience, not mere awareness.* Found in ~5 passes (ds-00 "advocacy," ds-01 "promote," ds-06 "promotion," k-04 "persuasion"). Weak; several slices contained no promotional acts.

**14. environment** *(weak)* — Sustain or improve natural resources, land, energy, and ecological conditions. *Discriminator vs. artifacts: the object is a natural system, not a made thing.* Found in only 2 passes (ds-02, ds-07). Genuine root, thinly exercised.

## B. Merges

- **care** ← care, heal, health, healthcare, healing, welfare, safeguarding, feed (ds-01), hospitality (ds-07)
- **safety** ← safety, protect (ds-01 fires), protecting (k-00, harm side)
- **security** ← security, privacy, access (ds-06), protecting (misuse side)
- **knowledge** ← knowledge, information, discovery, inquiry, investigation, insight, study (research side), documentation (k-03, insofar as records serve future knowing)
- **education** ← education, teaching, learning, study (instruction side)
- **communication** ← communication, inform, informing, circulation (k-02), expression (k-02/k-03, publication side), publishing
- **coordination** ← coordination, scheduling, planning, administer/administration (coordination side)
- **compliance** ← compliance, integrity, accountability, verifying, quality (ds-00)
- **justice** ← justice, legal, adjudication, dispute, resolving (k-00, dispute side)
- **commerce** ← commerce, finance, exchange, transaction, procurement, staffing (ds-02, loosely)
- **artifacts** ← production, making, build, construction, engineering, development, maintenance, repair, operations (physical side), logistics, move, moving, transport, creation, reliability (k-06, system-upkeep side)
- **membership** ← govern, order, moderation, membership, participation, governance (community side)
- **promotion** ← advocacy, promote, promotion, persuasion, publicity
- **environment** ← environment (ds-02, ds-07), sustain land/energy

## C. Dropped

- **representation / acting-for / liaison / delegation** (flagged unplaced in nearly every pass: ds-01, ds-02, ds-03, ds-05, ds-06, ds-07, k-01, k-05, k-06) — a standing relation between actors, not an end; excluded by rule. kimi-01's "representation" group was wrongly retained there and is dropped here.
- **operating / data stewardship / reliability / documentation-as-mechanism / CRUD "get/insert object"** (k-00 "operating" 32 acts, k-04 "data stewardship," ds-06's large mechanism unplaced block) — mechanisms, not purposes; the end is always whatever the platform or record serves.
- **operations / administration / planning** where separable from coordination — "keep the enterprise running" is a means-stack with no terminal end of its own; the coordination residue is absorbed into root 7.
- **diplomacy** (ds-07, 1 act), **staffing** (ds-02, 2 acts) — slice artifacts; absorbed into commerce/membership.
- **matchmaking / union / kinship / matching** (ds-01, ds-02, k-01, k-02, k-03; ~5 passes) — genuine ultimate end, but consistently 2–8 acts and domain-specific (marriage brokering slices); judged a slice artifact of a matchmaking-heavy corpus segment rather than a general root. Noted for a general taxonomy.
- **sentiment formation, being defeated, arriving at work** — internal states or outcomes, not acts toward an end.

## D. Weak Roots

- **promotion** (5 passes) — real root, but absent from most slices; some passes folded it into communication or commerce.
- **environment** (2 passes) — real root, almost unexercised.
- **membership** is moderate (~9 passes) but unstable in boundary with security and compliance; retained because the discriminating question is clean.

## E. What This Corpus Cannot Tell You

- **Play, leisure, worship, and aesthetic experience** — acts done for their own sake appear only as ds-05's 4-act "social"; recreation, ritual, and spirituality are essentially unexercised.
- **Kinship and reproduction** — forming households, raising children, care of family as such (the matchmaking acts are commercial brokering, not family life).
- **Subsistence** — growing, hunting, preparing food for one's own consumption (ds-01 "feed" is commercial food service).
- **Personal self-development** outside formal education (fitness, practice, reflection as ends).
- **Political self-governance** — voting, legislating, constituting authority; "governance" here is only moderation of platforms.
- **Matching/pairing as a general end** (see C) — the corpus hints at it but only through one domain.
- Whether **coordination** is truly terminal or always instrumental — the passes themselves disagreed (ds-03 unplaced it; ten others grouped it); this corpus cannot settle it.
