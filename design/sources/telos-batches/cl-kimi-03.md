## Groups

**healing** — preserving or restoring health and life, human or animal. (5) S0402, S0426, S0472

**protection** — preventing harm to people, assets, data, spaces, and communities, whether from accident, malice, or neglect. (11) S0451, S0456, S0504

**justice** — establishing facts and enforcing legal order so rightful outcomes are reached. (9) S0441, S0461, S0495

**integrity** — refusing to breach principle: keeping claims, accounts, models, and representations trustworthy. (7) S0417, S0443, S0531

**matching** — forming a fitting union between a person and a role, partner, or resource (jobs, marriages, aid). (8) S0453, S0514, S0480

**repair** — restoring or maintaining the function of artifacts and equipment. (7) S0439, S0485, S0507

**inquiry** — producing new knowledge through research, measurement, and reflection on failure. (5) S0448, S0493, S0512

**teaching** — building the capability, skill, or understanding of others. (3) S0424, S0455, S0530

**care** — attending directly to a person's bodily needs, comfort, dignity, or emotional wellbeing. (6) S0407, S0422, S0463

**privacy** — controlling what of oneself remains visible or retained by others. (3) S0431, S0450, S0492

**scheduling** — ordering commitments in time and governing access to a person's attention. (11) S0410, S0481, S0521

**communication** — making information reach the people for whom it matters. (14) S0460, S0506, S0520

**documentation** — creating and preserving records that can be found and trusted later. (10) S0400, S0446, S0513

**engineering** — conceiving, building, and keeping software and technical systems working. (11) S0427, S0479, S0519

**operations** — keeping the enterprise running: supplies, money, goods, and coordinated work flowing to their destinations. (14) S0404, S0457, S0518

## Unplaced

- **S0409** — being defeated is an outcome, not an act done for an end.
- **S0420** — "leave an object" states no discernible end (abandon? deposit?).
- **S0415, S0421, S0436, S0459, S0469, S0471, S0528, S0529, S0533, S0535** — retrieval/utility primitives; only mechanism is specified, the end they serve is unstated.
- **S0462** — serves state ceremony/protocol (diplomatic honor); no peer acts exist to form a group.
- **S0470** — maintains a grant of authority (governance); no peer acts exist to form a group.
