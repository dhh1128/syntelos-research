## Unplaced

- S0135 (accept a mandate), S0138 (decline a delegate), S0160 (represent a candidate), S0161 (convey refusal), S0168 (refuse to determine purposes), S0194 (refuse to action a case), S0241, S0221, S0176, S0146 — these are accept/decline/protocol moves: speech acts whose telos depends entirely on context; no ultimate end survives stripping.
- S0147 (form one's opinion) — an internal cognitive event, not an act directed at an external end.
- S0170/S0196 (arrive at / announce arrival at work) — mere presence/reporting; purpose is wholly instrumental to whatever work follows.
- S0175 (listen an object), S0186 (create an object), S0236 (discover an object), S0248 (remove an object), S0250 (commit to an object), S0229 (reserve a concrete object) — generic CRUD-style verbs over "object"; mechanism without specifiable end.
- S0243 (protect rights in sexual expression) — arguably advocacy, but the end (rights protection) is a value stance rather than a purpose the act itself achieves.
