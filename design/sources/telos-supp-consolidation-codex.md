## B. Nearly covered — defects in existing tests

- **judge:** The justice/adjudication groups include uncontested probate, declarations of status or validity, administrative determinations, penalties, and enforcement of already-settled obligations. “Contested claim” excludes these even though the same authoritative procedure fixes parties’ legal position. Narrowest repair: **“Does an authorized procedure determine a claim, legal status, liability, remedy, or sanction in a way that binds affected parties?”** The discriminant remains authoritative binding determination, not fact-finding, filing, rulemaking, or protection.

- **protect:** Safety/enforcement groups include firefighting, rescue, emergency response, hazard removal, and containment after a barrier has failed; these are not naturally “install, monitor, or enforce a barrier.” Add **“or deploy a countermeasure”**: “Does the act install, monitor, enforce, or deploy a countermeasure against an identified hazard?” Identified hazard still separates it from general care and maintenance.

- **communicate:** Public publishing, broadcasting, exhibitions, and open media access lack an individually “identified audience,” although transmission is complete at publication. Replace that phrase with **“identified or publicly delimited audience”**; this does not admit private drafting or mere record creation.

- **provide:** Live performances, events, continuously operated services, and utilities are outputs but not comfortably “artifacts.” Change “artifact” to **“artifact, production, facility, or service-capability.”** Require that the act itself create the production or make the capability operational; this avoids swallowing care, communication, and coordination merely because they are services.

## C. Not covered — candidate roots

### govern

Acts that constitute or exercise collective authority by making, authorizing, funding, or repealing rules, offices, budgets, and public programs. The determined outcome is the authoritative institutional arrangement or decision itself, not later compliance or public approval.

**Outward test:** “Does the act authoritatively constitute or alter a collective rule, office, budget, program, or allocation of public institutional power?”

**Discriminating question from judge:** Is the act setting or directing the common institutional framework, rather than binding parties by determining a claim, status, liability, remedy, or sanction?

All ten passes independently found it, normally as `governance`, `govern`, or `regulation`. Examples: **X0043, X0268, X0666**.

Tests tried: care changes no living condition directly; protect requires an identified hazard; know only represents facts; communicate only transmits the decision; coordinate fixes arrangements among parties but does not explain their authoritative force; provide concerns artifacts/capabilities; transfer covers budget movements but not the decision authorizing them; conform tests against an already external standard; judge determines cases rather than setting collective direction; belong changes bounded-group standing, not rules or public programs. Widening judge would erase the rulemaking/adjudication boundary; widening coordinate would erase the authority/arrangement boundary.

## D. Rejected candidates

- **influence/persuasion/promotion beyond the existing promote child:** killed by the **horizon** screen. Attention, support, trust, and changed choice depend on another party’s uptake; observable transmission remains communicate/promote.
- **connection/community/engagement:** desired affiliation is often a horizon. Actual messaging, meetings, membership changes, and role arrangements are communicate, coordinate, or belong.
- **autonomy:** usually a value served by privacy barriers, property dispositions, consent, or membership choices—not a separately determined outcome.
- **accountability/documentation/recordkeeping:** know or conform; recordkeeping is also a recurring mechanism.
- **property/estate/succession:** transfer and belong cover disposition and legal standing; preservation of the asset is provide/protect. “Property” is a subject area.
- **staffing/selection:** role assignment is coordinate; admission to a workforce or office is belong.
- **maintenance/continuity/infrastructure/administration:** provide, or mechanisms serving another end.
- **creation/performance/recreation:** creation is provide; presentation to an audience also communicates. Amusement as a mental reaction fails the horizon screen.
- **representation/counsel/mediation/matchmaking:** acting for another is a standing relation; arranging meetings or introductions is coordinate. A hoped-for agreement or marriage is a horizon unless the act itself formally unites.
- **transgression/subversion/exploitation:** illegality, evasion, coercion, or breach characterizes means or constraints; the ultimate gain or escape is frequently unstated. Where value actually moves, transfer already applies.
- **prosperity:** economic benefit is commonly a later aggregate consequence; concrete acts are trade, transfer, or provide.
- **funerary preparation:** isolated and underdetermined; embalming may be care-like bodily treatment, provision of a prepared body, ritual preparation, or compliance depending on context.

## E. What this corpus still cannot tell you

This is predominantly legal, administrative, service, and institutional material. It does not adequately test:

- **worship or ritual observance:** liturgies, sacrifices, vows, pilgrimages, and rites performed because the observance itself is required.
- **biological reproduction and cultivation:** mating, breeding, planting, incubation, and propagation corpora would test whether bringing forth living organisms fits provide or requires a distinct treatment.
- **consumption and sensory enjoyment:** eating, resting, solitary play, and aesthetic contemplation would test purposes centered on the actor’s immediately realized experience rather than audience reaction.
- **destruction and disposal as final outcomes:** demolition, euthanasia, waste destruction, decommissioning, and deliberate data erasure would test whether termination is merely the inverse phase of provide/care or independently discriminable.
- **exploration without a representational objective:** wilderness expeditions and open-ended experimentation would distinguish movement/experience from know.
- **military conquest and surrender:** operational war records would test changes in territorial authority that are neither ordinary protect nor ordinary govern.
