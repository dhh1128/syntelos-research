# A. The partition

The roots below partition all 1,076 acts by their most immediate stated purpose. When an act could support many downstream purposes but supplied no purpose-bearing context, I treated it as unplaceable rather than inferring one from the mechanism.

## `sensemaking`

**Definition.** Reduce uncertainty by obtaining, observing, interpreting, or organizing information about an existing or possible state of affairs. The intended outcome is understanding; no particular claim must yet be certified and no intervention selected.

**Discriminating question against `assuring`:** Is the act trying to learn what is true, or to establish that something satisfies a specified criterion or evidential standard?

**Count:** 175

- **S0006** — get elevation data for locations on the earth
- **S0184** — examine fire sites and collect evidence such as glass, metal fragments, charred wood, and accelerant residue for use in determining the cause of a fire
- **S0279** — examine patients to obtain information about their physical condition
- **S0504** — analyze areas of potential risk to the assets, earning capacity, or success of organizations
- **S0701** — examine physical evidence, such as hair, biological fluids, fiber, wood, or soil residues to obtain information about its source and composition

## `assuring`

**Definition.** Establish justified confidence that a claim, record, product, control, account, or implementation corresponds to an authoritative criterion. Testing, reconciliation, audit, substantiation, and evidence-based refusal belong here when their reason for existing is warranted correctness.

**Discriminating question against `sensemaking`:** Would success merely yield more information, or would it justify saying that a requirement, assertion, or result is sound?

**Count:** 102

- **S0001** — refuse to close with an unreconciled account
- **S0063** — verify a fix
- **S0137** — perform quality control audits to ensure accuracy, completeness, or proper usage of clinical systems and data
- **S0714** — run the three-way match
- **S0898** — test control design and operating effectiveness

## `making`

**Definition.** Realize an intended material, digital, spatial, or expressive state by creating, transforming, moving, operating, or finishing something. The resulting artifact, arrangement, location, or output—not increased knowledge or repaired capability—is the reason for the act.

**Discriminating question against `restoring`:** Is the act realizing a new intended state, or returning an impaired thing to an acceptable prior capability?

**Count:** 186

- **S0002** — build jigs that can be used as guides for assembling oversized or special types of box shooks
- **S0029** — develop detailed design drawings and specifications for mechanical equipment, dies, tools, and controls, using computer-assisted drafting (cad) equipment
- **S0202** — set up and operate computer editing systems, electronic titling systems, video switching equipment, and digital video effects units to produce a final product
- **S0324** — fabricate or form parts, coils, or structures according to specifications, using drills, calipers, cutters, or saws
- **S1023** — manipulate plot, score, sound, and graphics to make the parts into a continuous whole

## `restoring`

**Definition.** Recover, preserve, or stabilize a capability or condition threatened by wear, defect, depletion, disorder, or active failure. Maintenance, repair, replenishment, remediation, incident mitigation, and cleaning belong here when their telos is continued or recovered fitness for use.

**Discriminating question against `making`:** Does success create the next desired state, or remove degradation so an expected state can continue or return?

**Count:** 84

- **S0083** — close a ticket with a verified resolution
- **S0190** — diagnose and resolve media system problems
- **S0303** — repair parts or equipment
- **S0631** — drive to mitigation
- **S0927** — track postmortem action items to closure

## `coordinating`

**Definition.** Align actors, timing, attention, dependencies, conversations, or workflow stages so interdependent activity can proceed. Its outcome is organized participation rather than a grant of authority, substantive judgment, or faithful representation of a principal.

**Discriminating question against `authorizing`:** Is the act arranging how participants proceed, or determining whether someone is entitled to proceed?

**Count:** 126

- **S0015** — schedule tests or procedures for patients, such as lab work or x-rays, based on physician orders
- **S0136** — prepare agendas and make arrangements, such as coordinating catering for luncheons, for committee, board, and other meetings
- **S0390** — capture and chase meeting action items
- **S0497** — coordinate activities with other departments to ensure that services are provided in an efficient and timely manner
- **S0629** — coordinate incident roles

## `authorizing`

**Definition.** Confer, withhold, revoke, activate, or validate permission, consent, membership, delegated power, or binding approval. The desired result is a change—or confirmation—in who may participate, access, commit, or act.

**Discriminating question against `governing`:** Is this deciding a particular actor’s standing to act, or applying the substantive rules governing what conduct is acceptable?

**Count:** 69

- **S0011** — verify identity before a sensitive account action
- **S0183** — obtain sign-off for controlled spend
- **S0294** — grant access
- **S0781** — revoke access when in doubt
- **S0857** — provision exactly the access that was approved

## `governing`

**Definition.** Preserve legitimate, safe, fair, proportionate, or policy-conforming conduct by setting, interpreting, applying, escalating, or enforcing constraints. Its outcome is behavior kept within a normative boundary, including privacy, safety, legal, risk, and moderation boundaries.

**Discriminating question against `assuring`:** Is the act establishing whether a requirement is met, or deciding and effecting what the requirement permits, forbids, or demands?

**Count:** 94

- **S0025** — follow strict safety procedures when handling toxic materials to avoid contamination
- **S0126** — escalate a novel policy case
- **S0691** — apply a proportionate action
- **S0808** — apply a proportionate content-enforcement action
- **S0847** — scope and map controls to requirements

## `caring`

**Definition.** Improve or protect a person’s or animal’s health, functioning, welfare, development, dignity, or sustaining relationships. The beneficiary’s condition is the end, rather than an organizational output or a rule-compliance state.

**Discriminating question against `developing`:** Is the act improving a beneficiary’s lived condition, or building transferable capability and knowledge in a learner or organization?

**Count:** 64

- **S0017** — assist in locating housing for displaced individuals
- **S0050** — perform minor facial rejuvenation procedures, including the use of botox and laser technology
- **S0144** — counsel or aid family members to assist them in understanding, dealing with, or supporting the client or patient
- **S0458** — encourage and support the person in keeping contact with family and friends
- **S0957** — diagnose and treat psychiatric populations for conditions such as somatoform disorder, dementias, and psychoses

## `representing`

**Definition.** Advance, express, negotiate, or safeguard another party’s interests by acting in that party’s name, stead, or entrusted relationship. Fidelity to the principal—not merely successful communication or coordination—is constitutive of success.

**Discriminating question against `coordinating`:** Could the act be performed neutrally for all participants, or does it derive its purpose from loyalty to and authority from a particular principal?

**Count:** 57

- **S0208** — negotiate the client's contractual rights with third parties
- **S0237** — answer the media for the principal, or nominate who may speak for them
- **S0378** — advocate publicly for the person one represents
- **S0622** — represent the principal faithfully
- **S0936** — represent the sending State in the receiving State

## `exchanging`

**Definition.** Transfer, settle, procure, or account for value, goods, services, obligations, or reciprocal consideration between parties. Its end is completed exchange or faithful stewardship of exchange-related value, rather than mere movement or authorization.

**Discriminating question against `making`:** Is relocation or transformation itself the result, or is it instrumental to satisfying an economic or reciprocal obligation?

**Count:** 45

- **S0302** — pay an invoice
- **S0344** — pay a matched and authorized invoice
- **S0640** — participate in an exchange of goods and services for monetary compensation
- **S0899** — negotiate settlements of civil disputes
- **S1022** — take money from a buyer in exchange for goods or services rendered

## `developing`

**Definition.** Build durable knowledge, skill, judgment, design capability, or improved practice through teaching, research, experimentation, planning, or guided evaluation. Its product is enhanced future capacity rather than resolution of the immediate case being studied.

**Discriminating question against `sensemaking`:** Is knowledge sought to understand this object or case, or to create reusable capability, methods, designs, or competence for future action?

**Count:** 40

- **S0008** — instruct interns or residents in diagnosis and treatment of dermatological diseases
- **S0115** — train managers or supervisors on topics related to labor relations
- **S0650** — apply theoretical expertise and innovation to create or apply new technology
- **S0747** — engage in research to develop new photographic procedures and materials
- **S1002** — conduct research into the feasibility, design, operation, or performance of robotic mechanisms, components, or systems

The placed counts total **1,042**; the remaining **34** are below.

# C. Stress points

1. **S0037 — educate the public about health issues or enforce health legislation to prevent disease...**  
   Competitors: `developing` / `governing`. “Educate” builds capability, while “enforce” constrains conduct; the explicit terminal purpose—preventing disease—also pulls toward `caring`. I placed it in `governing` because the combined act describes population-level health protection through a public regime, but the sentence itself conflates three purposes.

2. **S0067 — collect background information about one client for disclosure to the other**  
   Competitors: `sensemaking` / `representing`. Collection reduces the broker’s uncertainty, but disclosure in service of the clients is the declared destination. I placed it in `representing`; the corpus does not say whether collection would still matter if no representation followed.

3. **S0132 — confirm a settlement**  
   Competitors: `assuring` / `exchanging`. “Confirm” suggests verification, while “settlement” suggests completion of an obligation or reciprocal resolution. I placed it in `exchanging`, but the act omits whether confirmation makes the settlement binding or merely checks its status.

4. **S0212 — evaluate new testing and sampling methodologies or technologies to determine usefulness**  
   Competitors: `sensemaking` / `developing`. It learns whether methods are useful, but that knowledge can create reusable testing capability. I placed it in `developing`; the corpus does not identify whether the evaluation concerns a one-off procurement choice or methodological advancement.

5. **S0256 — determine extent of liability according to evidence, laws, or administrative or judicial precedents**  
   Competitors: `sensemaking` / `governing`. The act produces a finding, but the finding is irreducibly normative. I placed it in `governing`; this exposes that determining what a rule implies lies between knowing the world and applying a rule to it.

6. **S0448 — run a blameless postmortem**  
   Competitors: `sensemaking` / `restoring`. A postmortem explains failure after service has been restored, yet exists to prevent recurrence and improve reliability. I placed it in `restoring` because operational recovery includes stabilization against recurrence, although the immediate output is knowledge.

7. **S0480 — match individuals' needs and eligibility with available financial aid programs to provide informed recommendations**  
   Competitors: `caring` / `sensemaking`. Matching and recommendation are epistemic, but the reason for them is access to welfare support. I placed it in `caring`; the corpus does not report whether the role is neutral eligibility administration or beneficiary advocacy.

8. **S0571 — design or conduct environmental restoration studies**  
   Competitors: `developing` / `restoring`. A study produces reusable knowledge or a plan; “restoration” names the condition ultimately sought. I placed it in `developing`, but the compound “design or conduct” does not say whether the work itself restores anything.

9. **S0649 — perform administrative functions, such as reviewing or writing reports, approving expenditures, enforcing rules, or purchasing...**  
   Competitors: `coordinating` / `governing` (with additional pressure from `authorizing` and `exchanging`). This is an aggregation of independently purposed acts and therefore resists any telic partition. I placed it in `coordinating` because administrative integration is the only purpose common to the list, but the corpus genuinely underspecifies it.

10. **S1073 — test and adjust communication and alarm systems, and report malfunctions to maintenance units**  
    Competitors: `assuring` / `restoring`. Testing establishes fitness, adjustment restores it, and reporting initiates somebody else’s repair. I placed it in `restoring` because an operational system is the terminal outcome, but the sentence bundles three stages that would separate if represented as atomic acts.

These are not merely difficult examples. They reveal the main weakness in the corpus: many entries are compound occupational duties, while many software entries are extremely thin protocol operations. Mutual exclusivity is consequently obtained by assigning the purpose of the entire stated act, not by pretending each grammatical verb has the same telos.

# D. What the corpus is missing

The corpus cannot establish several purposes that a general taxonomy would probably need:

- **Mourning or commemorating.** Funerals and deaths appear as matters to organize, certify, or notify, but no act exists for remembering or grieving as an end.
- **Worshipping.** Religious education appears, but prayer, devotion, ritual observance, and communion with the sacred do not.
- **Forgiving or reconciling personally.** Disputes are adjudicated or settled; interpersonal pardon and restoration of trust are absent.
- **Parenting and kin-making.** Guardianship and family communication occur, but nurturing a child or constituting family life as an end does not.
- **Democratic self-rule.** Poll voting appears as a protocol operation, but deliberating or collectively governing a polity does not.
- **Exploring for its own sake.** Research is instrumental to knowledge, methods, or design; curiosity-led exploration without a further outcome is not represented.
- **Contemplating or meaning-making.** The corpus has analysis and emotional support, but no act whose purpose is existential interpretation, reflection, or wisdom.
- **Celebrating.** Events are planned and hosted for principals, yet celebration itself is never the beneficiary-facing purpose.
- **Resting.** Leisure and play appear, but sleep, recuperation, and deliberate non-action are absent.
- **Creating intimate attachment.** Matchmaking and social introductions facilitate contact, but loving, befriending, or sustaining mutual intimacy as an actor’s own purpose is absent.
- **Ecological flourishing as an intrinsic end.** Environmental work is framed through restoration studies, regulation, risk, resource use, or technical design—not the flourishing of non-human systems for their own sake.
- **Resisting or transforming institutions.** The corpus contains refusal, escalation, and enforcement inside established roles, but almost no act aimed at replacing an illegitimate institution or redistributing power.

Accordingly, this partition is exhaustive only over this corpus. It is not evidence that these eleven roots exhaust human action in general.
